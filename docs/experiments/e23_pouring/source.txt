The method of Lin et al. [17] only uses the estimated object pose and size for calculating container-tocontainer distance as the initial pouring point. Such a strategy sometimes fails due to an imperfect initial pouring
point, as the transparent bottleneck results in inaccurate object size estimation. Our method only use object pose information to calculate the liquid-to-container distance as feedback to adjust the pouring point, which is less influenced by
the inaccurate size estimation. However, AFA method performs poorly than [17], because the inaccurately estimated
mask of AFA provides the wrong visual feedback to adjust
the pouring point, making the liquid spill out of the target
container instead.
