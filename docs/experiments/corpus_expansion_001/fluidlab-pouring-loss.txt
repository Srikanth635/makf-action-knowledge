Transporting, Pouring, Gathering (Easy) and Gathering (Hard): These tasks use a single spatial
location as the goal and the loss is computed as: L = Σi kpi − pgoal k, which is summed over all
particles of the object(s) to be manipulated. For Pouring, we apply this loss between the particles
of the upper layer fluid and the ground location, with an additional loss attracting the particles of the
lower layer fluid to their initial positions.
